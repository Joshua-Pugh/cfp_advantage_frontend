Advantage Through Contextual Football Profiles

Football Should Be Evaluated In Context

Traditional statistics tell us what happened. Final scores, win-loss records, rankings, and box scores all provide valuable information, but they do not always explain how a game was actually played.

CFP Advantage uses play-by-play and drive-level data to build Contextual Football Profiles that help explain team strength, game control, matchup dynamics, schedule context, and season performance beyond the final scoreboard.

Rather than relying solely on polls, reputation, or historical perception, CFP Advantage focuses on what teams consistently demonstrate on the field.

The Starting Question

Let The Data Explain The Game

CFP Advantage began with a simple question: what if team strength could be measured from how football is actually played, rather than from polls, reputation, or final scores alone?

Some teams win comfortably while the underlying performance suggests a much closer contest. Other teams survive close games despite controlling large portions of the action. Over the course of a season, those differences can reveal important information about team quality.

That question led to the development of the ADV framework: a drive-level and play-by-play approach designed to evaluate football through context, control, and execution.

What Makes It Different

Multiple Lenses, One Football Profile

CFP Advantage evaluates performance through multiple contextual lenses, including:

Team Strength

How strong a team has played across the season.

Offense & Defense

How each side of the ball contributes to the full profile.

Schedule Context

How opponent quality shapes the read.

Control Consistency

How reliably a team creates meaningful game control.

Drive Conversion

How often control becomes points.

Matchup Intelligence

How two profiles compare in a specific game.

Detailed metric definitions are available in the CFP Advantage Guide.

Not A Poll

Context, Not Narrative

CFP Advantage is not intended to replace traditional polls, rankings, committee evaluations, or the human element of football discussion.

The platform serves a different purpose. It measures football performance through drive-level execution, game control, schedule context, and team-strength profiling.

Sometimes the model agrees with public perception. Sometimes it challenges it. The goal is not to force a narrative. The goal is to provide context.

Mission

Better Questions, Better Football Conversations

CFP Advantage was built to help football fans explore historical seasons, review game recaps, compare matchups, analyze playoff paths, and question what the scoreboard says.

Football will always involve uncertainty. No model can predict every outcome. But better questions lead to better conversations.

Question assumptions. Follow the evidence. Let the data explain the game.
