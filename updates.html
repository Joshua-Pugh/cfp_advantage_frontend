September 19, 2026

2026 Development Model

CFP Advantage retired the original frozen 2026 validation after live review exposed an early-season opponent-adjustment problem. The season now operates as a versioned development cycle so verified issues can be corrected between weekly releases.

Why The Frozen Validation Stopped

  • The early schedule graph could overreact to disconnected groups of teams and sparse two-game samples.
  • Lower-division opponents did not have stable context inside the original early-season solve.
  • The old universal scoreboard-margin translation was not calibrated to the corrected rating scale.

What Changed

  • The current rating solve uses a tested preseason prior, bounded game evidence, stable lower-division context, and a convergence check.
  • Future margins use a chronologically tested class-aware translation fitted to the corrected rating scale.
  • 2016–2026 remain the public data years; 2015 is retained only as historical model-development evidence.

What Remains Preserved

  • Weeks 1–3 receipts, grades, and CFBD Pick'em submissions remain exactly as published.
  • Core ADV game scoring and the Control Framework were not changed by the rating correction.
  • The ATS research lanes remain private and unchanged while their future results continue to be tracked.

September 3, 2026

CFP Advantage 1.1

This release sharpens the live-season experience and makes historical coverage easier to understand.

Team Profiles

  • Clarified opponent-first schedule rows and expanded team-logo coverage.
  • Added practical metric guidance to help readers compare team identities.
  • Established 2016 as the first public historical season with complete Control Framework coverage.

Matchups And Live Scores

  • Added completed-game status and score context to the weekly slate.
  • Limited the live board to five selected games for easier tracking.
  • Improved weekly-slate pagination and mobile layouts.

Site Experience

  • Introduced the ADV brand mark across site headers and browser icons.
  • Added a shareable Framework Card for turning team metrics into one concise football identity.
  • Made metric help available by tap as well as hover.
  • Moved Contact into a full responsive page.
