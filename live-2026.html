Schedule Note

Pac-12 Flex Week

The Pac-12 is using a late-season flex game in 2026. Those matchups are expected to be assigned near the end of the regular season and will count toward overall records, but not conference standings.

Until those games are set, CFP Advantage will label Pac-12 record-path outputs separately or hold them out of full record-probability views.

Model Validation

How CFP Advantage Earns Trust

CFP Advantage does not treat every idea as Product A. Each major layer is tested historically before it becomes part of the public model language. The goal is not to prove the model is perfect. The goal is to show the work, explain what survived, and track the 2026 season in public.

Projection Stack 71.37% Winner accuracy across 7,436 historical games
Margin Error 13.37 Mean absolute error in the final Product A audit
Control Framework Promoted Used to explain support, pushback, mixed reads, and conflicts
Pressure Metrics Validated Pressure buckets move with future scoring and scoring allowed

How We Test

  1. Start with a football question.
  2. Build a metric or context layer.
  3. Freeze inputs before kickoff.
  4. Test chronologically across past seasons.
  5. Promote only what survives.

What Did Not Become Core

Some ideas remained context-only or research-only because they did not improve the model enough in chronological testing. Recent Form, AP divergence, raw pressure-to-points translation, and direct Control Framework margin correction all stayed out of the official projection stack.

Model Validation

What Earned A Place In Product A

Every metric inside CFP Advantage began as a football question, not a feature. Before a metric becomes part of Product A, it has to show meaningful football value through historical testing. Some ideas become core model layers. Others remain context. Some are rejected entirely.

CFP Advantage does not ask users to trust a black box. It shows what was tested, what survived, and how each public layer should be used.
