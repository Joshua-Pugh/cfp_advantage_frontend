Reading Pressure vs Scoreboard

Pressure is the underlying scoring identity. Points are the scoreboard output.

Control Points Per Offensive Drive measures sustainable scoring pressure created per possession. Control Points Allowed Per Defensive Drive is the lower-is-better defensive mirror. These are not literal points per drive, but they help explain whether a team is creating or allowing repeatable scoring pressure.

Use pressure beside traditional stats like points per game, points allowed per game, red zone touchdown rate, and points per drive. When pressure and scoreboard output move in the same direction, the profile is closer to its underlying identity. When they differ, the scoreboard may be running hotter or colder than the true control profile.
