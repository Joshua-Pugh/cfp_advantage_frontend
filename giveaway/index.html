Road to 500 Giveaway

Official Rules

NO PURCHASE, PAYMENT, OR CONTRIBUTION NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE, PAYMENT, OR CONTRIBUTION WILL NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING.

1. Sponsor

The CFP Advantage Road to 500 Giveaway (the “Giveaway”) is sponsored by CFP Advantage (“Sponsor”). This Giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook, Meta, or any gift-card issuer.

2. Giveaway Period

The Giveaway begins on September 16, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on December 11, 2026 (“Giveaway Period”). All entries must be received during the Giveaway Period.

3. Eligibility

Open to legal residents of the 50 United States and District of Columbia who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Employees, contractors, individuals directly involved in administering the Giveaway, and members of their immediate households are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

4. How to Enter

During the Giveaway Period, visit cfpadvantage.com/giveaway and complete and submit the official Giveaway entry form. Following CFP Advantage on Facebook is not required. Likes, comments, shares, follows, donations, purchases, and other social-media activity do not constitute entries or provide additional entries. Limit one entry per person during the Giveaway Period.

5. Road to 500 Community Campaign

The Road to 500 is a community-growth campaign with a goal of reaching 500 Facebook followers. The follower goal is separate from entry into the Giveaway. Reaching 500 followers is not a condition for awarding the prize. Commenting “500” on designated CFP Advantage Facebook posts may be used to receive campaign status through an automated response. Such a comment is not an entry and does not increase the chance of winning.

6. Prize

One eligible entrant will receive a gift card with a minimum value of $100 USD. Sponsor may voluntarily increase the prize based on qualifying community support received during the Giveaway Period: $0–$99.99 support, $100 prize; $100–$249.99, $125 prize; $250–$499.99, $175 prize; $500–$999.99, $250 prize; and $1,000 or more, $500 prize.

The final prize value will be determined from successfully processed support received before the end of the Giveaway Period, excluding refunded, reversed, disputed, or fraudulent transactions. No contribution is required to enter or win. The prize is non-transferable, and no substitution is permitted by the winner. Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if necessary. The winner is responsible for applicable taxes.

7. Community Support

Financial support of CFP Advantage is entirely voluntary and is not an entry method. Supporting CFP Advantage does not provide an additional entry, improve the odds of winning, or influence winner selection. Community support may increase the prize available to all eligible entrants according to the announced prize milestones.

8. Founders Recognition

Individuals who voluntarily support CFP Advantage may choose to have their name permanently recognized on the CFP Advantage Founders Page planned for Version 2. Recognition is optional, is separate from the Giveaway, and provides no additional entry, advantage, preference, or increased odds. Sponsor may decline names or content that are fraudulent, misleading, offensive, impersonate another person, or are inappropriate for public display.

9. Winner Selection

After the Giveaway Period ends, one potential winner will be selected at random from all eligible unique entries received through the official entry form. The odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received.

10. Winner Notification

The potential winner will be contacted using the email address submitted on the entry form and must respond within seven calendar days. If the potential winner cannot be contacted, is ineligible, does not respond in time, or declines the prize, Sponsor may randomly select an alternate potential winner. CFP Advantage will never require a winner to make a payment or contribution to receive the prize.

11. Verification

Sponsor may verify an entrant's eligibility and compliance with these Official Rules. Automated, fraudulent, duplicate, incomplete, manipulated, or otherwise invalid entries may be disqualified.

12. Privacy

Information submitted through the entry form will be used to administer the Giveaway, contact the potential winner, prevent duplicate or fraudulent entries, and fulfill obligations associated with the promotion. Entry will not automatically enroll entrants in marketing emails unless they separately and affirmatively opt in. See the CFP Advantage Privacy Policy.

13. General Conditions

Sponsor may suspend, modify, extend, or cancel the Giveaway if fraud, technical failures, legal requirements, platform failures, events beyond Sponsor's reasonable control, or other circumstances materially affect its integrity or administration. If terminated early, Sponsor may select a winner from eligible entries received before termination where lawful and appropriate. Sponsor's administration decisions are final, subject to applicable law.

14. Social Media Disclaimer

This Giveaway is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook or Meta. By entering, participants acknowledge that Giveaway information is being provided to CFP Advantage and not Facebook or Meta, and release Facebook and Meta from claims arising from or related to the Giveaway.

Questions should be directed to support@cfpadvantage.com.
